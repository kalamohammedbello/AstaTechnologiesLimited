About us

Who we are

Asta Technologies Limited is a Nigerian digital design and technology company registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). We help businesses, organisations and individuals build a strong, professional presence online through thoughtful design and dependable technology.

By keeping web, brand, product and social media design under one roof, we make sure every touchpoint a customer sees looks and feels like it belongs to the same brand.

Our Vision

To be a trusted name in African digital design, known for work that helps brands grow with confidence.

Our Mission

To deliver creative, reliable and affordable design and technology solutions that turn ideas into strong brands.
